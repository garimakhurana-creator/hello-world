Campaign

The Ivory Edit

Travertine, raw silk and a single red shield. We shot the Kamal Shield in the midday sun because that's where it lives: at the wedding in Udaipur, on the terrace in Lutyens' Delhi, on the promenade in Cannes.

The lotus plaque on each hinge is cast in solid brass and hand-polished for four hours before it's gilded.

How a pair is made

Forty days, fourteen hands

  1. 01

    The motif

    Each design begins as a drawing from a temple frieze, a palace jaali or a miniature painting.

  2. 02

    The cast

    Motifs are carved in wax and lost-wax cast in brass, the method used for Chola bronzes.

  3. 03

    The setting

    Stones are hand-set in kundan style. The Heirloom Edit uses lab-grown rubies and emeralds.

  4. 04

    The finish

    Italian acetate is polished by hand. Every pair carries a serial number and a certificate of craft.
