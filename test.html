Who I Am

Beyond the platform and the promises, I want you to know the person you'd actually be voting for. I lead with a firm sense of direction, but I never lose the warmth that makes people feel like they can come to me with anything — big or small, any hour of the day.

What Drives Me

🧭

Firm & Grounded

I make decisions with conviction and stand by them. People know where they stand with me, and that steadiness is something I bring to every room I'm in.

🫂

A Comfortable Presence

I want people to feel at ease around me — no pretense, no judgment. Whether it's a quick hello or a deep conversation, I try to make space feel safe.

🌙

Always Available

I'm the person you can call at 3 AM when something's on your mind. Being there when it actually matters — not just during office hours — is non-negotiable for me.

💛

Led by Empathy

I try to understand where people are coming from before I react. Empathy isn't just a value I hold — it's how I want WISE to feel for every single member.

"Leadership isn't about being the loudest voice in the room — it's about being the person people know they can count on, any time, day or night."
